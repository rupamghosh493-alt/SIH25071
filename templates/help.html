Frequently Asked Questions

3 TOPICS
01 What is Factor of Safety? +

Factor of Safety is a stability indicator. Lower values generally indicate greater concern and should be reviewed alongside the other monitored parameters.

02 Can I upload my own data? +

Yes. Administrators can use Data Ingestion to upload field observations in CSV format. The required columns are shown on the Data Upload page.

03 Is MindShield360 production-ready? +

No. This application is a prototype intended for demonstration and hackathon use. Validated geotechnical models, calibrated sensors and professional safety procedures are required before real-world deployment.
